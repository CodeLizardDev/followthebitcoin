# Quest evidence and simplification policy

Real-case quests teach how to assess evidence. They distinguish public
transactions, outside records, inferences about people and authority to spend.
Privacy is useful for ordinary people; use of a privacy tool is not evidence of
criminal conduct by itself.

## Repeatable authoring and review

1. State one learning goal for each stage. Record what the observer knows before
   asking the question. Give each question one supported conclusion and explain
   why the other conclusions go beyond the evidence.
2. Use primary sources: court documents, official findings, admitted facts and
   dated agency announcements. Keep the source URL, date and relevant page or
   paragraph beside the claim. Check later pleas, judgments and corrections
   before publishing a historical accusation as an established outcome.
3. Attribute an investigator's affidavit to the investigator. Distinguish its
   evidence descriptions from later admissions and convictions. Name the actual
   offense of conviction; do not substitute a broader headline description.
4. Label reconstructed diagrams and invented labels clearly. Mark selected or
   compressed flows as such. A service's name or account holder is supplied
   context unless the player has evidence establishing it. Do not invent a
   complete public route across services from a partial official diagram.
5. Keep identity, technical control and legal entitlement distinct. Avoid claims
   that an address proves a person's identity, a private key proves rightful
   ownership, or tracing alone provides the ability to seize bitcoin.
6. Use historical dates for valuations and outcomes. A sentence imposed on a
   given date does not establish current custody. Do not claim that seized assets
   have been returned unless a source confirms the specific distribution.
7. Keep game data fictional or illustrative. Never request real wallet details,
   identity documents or keys. If numerical transactions are introduced, use
   integer satoshis and check inputs = outputs + fee. Keep both the interactive
   quest and its no-JavaScript lesson consistent with the sources.

Reviewers should be able to inspect every source from the relevant reveal and
the final case summary. A completion badge records finishing the lesson; it
does not certify a player's knowledge, financial privacy or legal status.

## Quest 02: The Locked Fortune

Source review: **2026-10-16**. The three exhibits compress a much larger
investigation. Their routes and labels are teaching illustrations; the exhibits
do not reproduce a complete transaction graph.

The affidavit's paragraph 6 describes the seizure's legal chronology, including
exigent-circumstances authorization and a subsequent seizure warrant. The lesson
therefore distinguishes lawful authority from signing capability without
presenting every search warrant as a prior seizure warrant.
